Different weather conditions require completely different recipes. Buy Research to find the optimal Temperature, Sweetness, and Intensity for each weather type.

2. Economics & Pricing

Your Unit Cost is what you pay to brew one cup. Your Margin is your Price minus your Unit Cost. You pay $150 in Rent daily, so sell enough to cover costs!

If you get a Taste Score of 75+ and make a profit, your Buzz streak increases, giving you permanent extra daily customers. A bad day resets your streak to zero!

4. Corporate Espionage

📐 The Math Guide

Taste Score follows a quadratic curve. Finding the peak (Vertex) maximizes profit.

Tier 1: Raw Data (Secant Lines)

You get two "ugly" coordinates close together. If the y-value is increasing, the slope is positive, meaning you haven't reached the peak yet. Move the slider right!

Tier 2: Symmetry

You get three points. Two have the exact same y-value. Because parabolas are perfectly symmetrical, the vertex is in the middle. Use the midpoint formula: (x₁ + x₂) / 2.

Tier 3: Standard Form

You are given P(x) = ax² + bx + c. To find the "Perfect Amount" (x), use the vertex formula:
x = -b / (2a)

Tier 4: Vertex Form

You are given P(x) = a(x - h)² + k.
h is the "Perfect Amount" for your slider. k is the Max Taste Score.

Finding the Roots (Sabotage)

To sabotage, you must give a recipe where Taste Score > 0. Set the equation to 0 = a(x - h)² + k and solve for the two x-intercepts (roots). Give a number between those roots!
